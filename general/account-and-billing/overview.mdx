---
title: "Account overview"
description: "Check your Gologin app version and change the interface language."
---

The **Overview** tab of your settings shows your account details and app settings. To open it, click your **workspace name** in the top-left corner > **Settings & Members** > **Overview**.

Here you can:

- see the **version of the Gologin app** you're using - useful when contacting support;
- change the **interface language** of the app: choose it in the language field.
