## How to invite team members

<Info>
  **Note:** if you choose to share the workspace access, the team member will have access to **all** the included profiles and folders.
</Info>

To start collaborating with your team and add members:

1. Click **Invite members** at the bottom-left
2. Enter the **teammate's email address**
3. Select the access type: **Workspace** or **Selected Folders**
4. Choose the role: **Can run**, **Can edit** or **Full access**
5. Click **Send invite**

<Frame>
  ![Invite Team Members](/images/invite_team_members-1.gif)
</Frame>

The invited person will receive an email invitation in their inbox.\
If they have questions about accessing the workspace, show them [this article](/general/team-collaboration/accessing-shared-workspace-or-folders).

## Inviting with a link

Instead of entering an email, you can send an invite link, for example in a messenger:

1. Click **Invite members** at the bottom-left
2. In the **Invite team members** window, click **Invite link** - the link is copied to your clipboard
3. Send the link to your teammate

Each link works only once and is valid for 24 hours. If it has expired or has already been used, create a new one. When someone joins your workspace with a link, you'll get an email about it.

If you see **You've reached the limit of team members**, add a team member seat first - see [Team Members](/general/team-collaboration/team-members).

## Changing and revoking access

To change or revoke the workspace access:

1. Navigate to the **main menu** at the top-left \> **Settings & Members**
2. **Locate the member** In the Settings & Members tab \> **click at their permission**
3. **Change the permission** as needed
4. To revoke the workspace access click **Remove member** in the access menu
