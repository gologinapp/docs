title: "Purchasing Gologin Proxies"
---

Gologin sells proxies in two ways:

- **Proxy traffic** in gigabytes, used by our residential, mobile, and datacenter proxies. Every paid plan already includes some.
- **Dedicated IP** - a single static IP address reserved for you, sold as a \$5/month subscription per address. This article covers both.

## Default proxy traffic

All paid plans include **2 GB of residential proxy traffic** per month, regardless of the plan tier or billing period (monthly or annual). This traffic refreshes at the start of each billing month. It does not carry over - any unused traffic from the previous month is replaced with a fresh 2 GB.

<Check>
  Annual subscribers also receive 2 GB per month, not 2 GB per year. The refresh happens every month for the entire duration of the subscription.
</Check>

## Buying additional traffic

### One-time top-up

A top-up is a one-time purchase of extra traffic. Use this if you have an occasional spike in usage and do not need more traffic every month.

**Steps:**

1. On the main **All profiles** page, click the traffic amount next to the **Proxies** tab in the left side-menu.
2. Select the **proxy type** you want to buy traffic for.
3. Choose the **amount of traffic** you need.
4. Click the **\+** button next to your chosen amount and proceed to checkout.

<Frame>
  ![Proxy Top Up](/images/proxy_top_up.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Prices shown in the app are the most up to date. Check them directly before purchasing.
</Info>

**Payment methods for top-ups:**

You can pay with a credit or debit card, or with crypto. Users from some countries - such as Russia and Belarus - may not be able to pay by card for top-ups. If your card payment is declined, use crypto instead.

### Subscription add-on

A subscription add-on adds a set amount of proxy traffic to your plan on a recurring basis. This means the traffic is added automatically every month.

You can add traffic to your subscription during initial checkout or at any time by changing your current plan.

**How to add traffic to your current plan:**

1. On the main **All profiles** page, click the **cog icon** next to your plan info at the bottom-left.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. On the pricing page, confirm your plan - you should see the **Renew the current plan** button, which means you are adding to your current plan rather than changing it.
4. Find the **proxy traffic** add-on and set the amount you want added each month.
5. Click **Upgrade now**, choose the payment method and click **Pay now** to complete checkout.

<Frame>
  ![Proxy With Subscription](/images/proxy_with_subscription.gif)
</Frame>

**How recurring traffic works:**

- **Monthly plan** - the add-on amount is charged and added every month when your subscription renews.
- **Annual plan** - the add-on is paid once as part of your annual subscription and added to your account every month throughout the year.

For more details on changing your plan, see [Change and upgrade subscription](/general/account-and-billing/change-plan).

## Buying a Dedicated IP

A **Dedicated IP** is a single static address that belongs only to you. It never rotates, it has no traffic limit, and it is the right choice for any account that needs to keep the same IP over time - Amazon, eBay, LinkedIn, Meta Ads, PayPal, betting platforms.

<Info>
  Dedicated IPs are available on paid plans only. They are not part of the free trial.
</Info>

### Steps

1. Click **Add profile**.
2. Open the **Proxy** tab and select **Gologin proxy**.
3. Click **Buy dedicated IP**.
4. Pick a country from the selector.
5. Click **Buy for \$5/month**.
6. Complete checkout in the billing window that opens. You can pay by card through Stripe or with crypto through Cryptomus.
7. When you return to Gologin, a confirmation window tells you the payment went through.
8. Wait **5-7 minutes** for the IP to be assigned to your account.

<Frame>
  ![Buying Dedicated IP](/images/buying_dedicated_IP.gif)
</Frame>

<Note>
  The price is always **\$5** - Gologin does not add any processing fees. If you pay with crypto, your own wallet or exchange may charge a network fee on top of the invoice. See [Payment methods](https://gologin.com/docs/general/account-and-billing/payment-methods) for details.
</Note>

### Finding your Dedicated IP after purchase

The IP is **not attached to any profile automatically**. After 5-7 minutes, refresh the page - you may need to reload for it to appear.

Your Dedicated IP will then be available in three places:

- The **Proxies** page, in the list of your own proxies
- The **Your proxy** tab on the **New Browser Profile** page
- The proxy manager on the **All profiles** page, when assigning a proxy to an existing profile

### How Dedicated IP billing works

A Dedicated IP is tied to your main Gologin subscription, so it always ends when your subscription ends.

| Your plan | What you pay |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | \$5, charged now, renewing with your plan each month |
| Annual, with months remaining | \$5 × the number of months left, charged now |

If your annual plan has an incomplete month left, it is billed as a full month.

**While your subscription is active and renewing, the IP stays the same.** You keep the same address until you cancel.

<Warning>
  If you pay with crypto, the Dedicated IP cannot renew automatically. It works for the period you paid for and then stops, and you will not be able to get the same IP back afterwards.
</Warning>

### Cancelling and refunds

- A Dedicated IP is cancelled together with your Gologin subscription. There is no separate cancellation for a single IP at the moment.
- If you cancel, the IP keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for.
- A Dedicated IP cannot be swapped for a different address.
- Refunds are available within **14 days** of purchase - contact support.

<Note>
  A Dedicated IP belongs to the account that bought it and is not shared with other workspace members. However, if you assign it to a profile and share that profile, anyone with access to the profile can use it.
</Note>
