Crypto exchanges and wallets (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken and others) tie each account to a real person through identity verification (KYC). After verification, the exchange watches where and how you sign in, so a sudden change of IP or device can trigger a security review, extra verification or a frozen account.

<Warning>
  Pass verification only with your own identity documents. Using another person's identity or documents is prohibited by the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://gologin.com/acceptable-use-policy/).
</Warning>

## Quick checklist

- 1 account = 1 browser profile
- 1 profile = 1 static residential proxy
- Keep the proxy location the same, especially after verification
- Don't change the fingerprint or the proxy after identity verification

## Gologin profile setup

- **Proxy:** static residential, the same IP for every session
- **Proxy location:** the country where you live and that is on your documents

<Warning>
  Don't change the proxy of the profile after you pass verification on an exchange. The exchange remembers your device and IP at verification time, and a different IP or fingerprint on the next sign-in can look like an account takeover.
</Warning>

## The first weeks of a new account

| **Period** | **Focus** | **What to do** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Day 0 | Registration | Create the account and confirm the email |
| Days 1–3 | Verification | Submit your identity documents and complete verification |
| Days 4–7 | Exploration | Browse the platform and the trading interface |
| Week 2 | Small transactions | Deposit a small amount, make 1–2 trades |
| Week 3\+ | Normal use | Regular trading activity |

## What triggers security checks

- An IP change after verification, even within the same country
- A device fingerprint that differs between sessions
- Large deposits right after the account is created
