In the [AI Assistant](/ai/ai-assistant), choose your connected accounts and describe the job in one sentence. The assistant turns the request into a plan: who to contact, how many people a day and what to write. Nothing is sent until you approve the plan.

## Example requests

- "Invite heads of growth at Nordic SaaS companies"
- "Find senior Python developers in Warsaw"
- "Follow up with people who accepted my invites"
- "Post about our launch and answer the comments"
- "Draft replies to new messages"

## What the assistant can do on LinkedIn

- **Search.** Find people by keywords or by a LinkedIn search link with all its filters, find companies and posts, read profiles and work experience.
- **Network.** Send invitations with a note, accept or ignore received invitations, list connections and see new ones.
- **Messages.** Write to new people and continue existing conversations. A paid InMail is never spent without your consent.
- **Posts.** Publish posts, react, comment and reply to comments, see who reacted and commented.

## How tasks run

- Tasks run in the cloud, so you can close the app or turn off your computer.
- The assistant stays within each account's [limits](/ai/connected-accounts/limits). When a limit is used up, the rest of the work moves to the next window instead of failing.
- A chat works with one tool: connected accounts or Gologin profiles. The accounts you choose in a chat are remembered for it.

## Inbox

**Inbox** collects conversations from all your connected accounts in one list, with the **All** and **Unread** filters.

- When you reply, the message goes out from the account the conversation came to.
- Conversations from before the connection appear only if you checked **Import existing conversations** when you connected the account.
- To get new messages in your own system, set up a webhook in the [API](/ai/connected-accounts/api#get-new-messages-on-a-webhook).
