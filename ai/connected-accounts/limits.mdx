Every connected account has its own limits for each type of action. A new account starts with low limits that grow every week, so it warms up the way a person's account would.

## How limits work

- Limits count every action from the account: from the AI Assistant chats, templates and the API.
- Daily limits count actions in the last 24 hours, weekly ones in the last 7 days, and the limit on invitations with a note in the last 30 days.
- When a limit is used up, the action waits for the next window. The task doesn't fail. The API answers with the limit and the time when it frees up.
- You can see the limits and how much of each is used under **Daily limits**. To change them, click **Limit settings**.

## From Week 1 to Full

- A new account starts at **Week 1** and moves to the next stage every 7 days from its first sign-in.
- After **Week 4**, the account moves to **Full** if in these 4 weeks LinkedIn didn't restrict it, at least 30% of its invitations were accepted and it has at least 150 connections. Otherwise, the check repeats in 7 days.
- If LinkedIn restricts invitations for an account, its invitations stop for 7 days, and Gologin suggests moving the account back to **Week 1**.

## Recommended limits

| Action | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 | Full |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Invitations per day | 5 | 8 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| Invitations per 7 days | 30 | 50 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
| Invitations with a note per 30 days | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Note length, characters | 200 | 200 | 200 | 200 | 200 |
| Messages to connections per day | 10 | 15 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
| Profile views per day | 50 | 80 | 100 | 150 | 200 |
| Profile views per 7 days | 300 | 500 | 600 | 800 | 1,000 |
| People search pages per day | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 10 |
| Posts per day | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Posts per 7 days | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 7 |
| Comments per day | 5 | 5 | 10 | 10 | 30 |
| Comments per 7 days | 30 | 30 | 50 | 50 | 150 |
| Reactions per day | 10 | 20 | 30 | 30 | 100 |
| Reactions per 7 days | 50 | 100 | 150 | 150 | 500 |

An account also keeps at most 200 pending invitations (500 at **Full**). An invitation that stays pending for 21 days is withdrawn.

## Change the limits

In **Limit settings**, you can:

- **Choose a stage.** For example, choose **Full** for an account you have used actively for a long time. The limits of that stage apply at once.
- **Set your own value** for any limit. Your values override the stage and stay when the stage changes.
- **Reset to recommended** to remove your own values.

The same settings are available in the [API](/ai/connected-accounts/api#limits).
