**Connected Accounts** (the **Accounts** section in the app) let you connect your LinkedIn accounts to Gologin once and then run outreach from them: search for people, send invitations and messages, answer replies, publish posts and comments.

Each connected account gets:

- its own Gologin browser profile with a unique fingerprint;
- a dedicated IP in the country you choose, or your own proxy;
- a session that stays signed in. Accounts work in the cloud, so tasks keep running when your computer is off.

A connected account is not the same as a browser profile. A profile is an isolated browser that you or the [AI Assistant](/ai/ai-assistant) work in. With a connected account, you give a task, and Gologin performs the actions through that account in its own profile.

![Ai Assistant Connected Accounts](/images/ai_assistant_connected_accounts.png "Ai Assistant Connected Accounts")

## Ways to work with your accounts

- **AI Assistant.** Describe the job in one sentence, for example "Invite heads of growth at SaaS companies in Berlin". The assistant shows a plan and sends nothing until you approve it. See [Outreach with the AI Assistant](/ai/connected-accounts/assistant-and-inbox).
- **Inbox.** Replies from all your accounts land in one list, and each answer goes out from the account the message came to.
- **API.** Run the same accounts from your own code or AI agent, within the same limits. See [Connected Accounts API](/ai/connected-accounts/api).

## Supported networks

**LinkedIn** is available now. Twitter/X, Reddit, Instagram, Gmail, WhatsApp and Telegram are coming soon.

## Seats and early access

Each connected account takes one seat in your workspace, whatever its status. Disconnecting an account keeps its seat. Only deleting the account frees the seat for another one. The app and the API share the same seats.

Connected Accounts is free during early access: you can connect up to 3 accounts per workspace, no card needed. To test more accounts, book a demo on the [Connected Accounts page](https://gologin.com/connected-accounts/).

## Where to find it

Click **Accounts** in the left menu of the Gologin app, or open the **AI Assistant** and use **Accounts**, **Inbox** and **API** under **Connected accounts**.
