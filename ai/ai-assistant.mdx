**AI Assistant** is a chat assistant built into the Gologin app. It can manage your browser profiles and work on websites inside a Gologin profile the way a regular user would - clicking, typing, filling in forms and scrolling.

To open it, click **AI Assistant** in the left menu.

![Ai Assistant](/images/ai_assistant.png "Ai Assistant")

## What you can ask it to do

**Manage profiles:**

- list your profiles and show profile details
- create, update, clone or delete profiles
- organize profiles into folders and add tags
- set up proxies and attach them to profiles
- get the cookies of a profile

**Work on websites in a profile:**

- start or stop a profile
- open websites and follow links
- read the visible page content
- click buttons, links and menus, type into fields, submit forms, scroll and select options

Example requests:

- "Create 10 profiles with US proxies and warm them up"
- "Move these profiles into a folder called Clients"
- "Open this website in the profile Research and collect the prices from the first page"

## Templates

Under **Chat and templates**, you can start from a ready-made scenario: **Batch Create Profiles**, and warm-up scenarios for **Facebook & Instagram**, **TikTok**, **YouTube**, **Shopping Simulation** and **News Reading**. A warm-up template creates a new profile with a proxy and browses the site without logging in, so the profile builds a natural browsing history before you use it.

## Writing good requests

The assistant acts only on what you ask. Give it a clear, specific task - which profile to use, which website to open and what to do there. It won't decide on its own what to post, like or comment.

## Limitations

- The assistant can't solve CAPTCHA, reCAPTCHA or other human checks, and can't pass login, email or two-factor verification. If one appears, complete it yourself in the profile, then let the assistant continue.
- Actions with serious consequences - purchases, payments, sending messages, deleting data, changing security settings or accepting agreements - are performed only with your clear confirmation.
